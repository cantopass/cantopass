Canto HK Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

The short version
  • Canto HK does not collect, store or share any personal data.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your voice is analysed on your phone to draw your pitch, and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your learning progress stays on your device, and in your own iCloud account if you use iCloud.

This policy explains how the Canto HK app ("the app") handles information. It applies to the app on iPhone and iPad.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect personal information, and the developer has no servers that receive data from it. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and no third-party software development kits.

The microphone

Some exercises ask you to say a word so the app can show your pitch against the target tone. When you tap the record button, the app asks for permission to use the microphone. The sound is analysed in memory on your device while you speak, to measure its pitch. The audio is not recorded to a file, not kept after the exercise, and never leaves your device. You can turn microphone access off at any time in Settings › Canto HK; the rest of the app keeps working.

Your learning progress

Your progress (lessons completed, experience points, streak, scores and settings) is saved on your device so the app remembers where you are.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy of this progress in your iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it survives reinstalling the app and appears on your other Apple devices. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see or access it. You can stop it by turning iCloud off for Canto HK in Settings › [your name] › iCloud, and you can erase your progress at any time with "Reset all progress" in the app's Me tab.

Audio in the app

The Cantonese speech you hear is pre-recorded and built into the app. Where no recording exists, the app uses the speech voice built into your device. Neither sends anything you do to anyone.

Purchases

If the app offers paid features, purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or other personal information from these purchases.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting any information, this policy will be updated before that happens.
