Common questions

The tone graph doesn't react to my voice. Check that microphone access is on in Settings › Canto HK, then speak clearly and close to the phone. Your voice is analysed on your phone and never recorded or sent anywhere.

I can't hear any audio. Turn the volume up and check the silent switch. The Cantonese recordings are built into the app, so they work offline.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app or change phones? If you're signed in to iCloud, your progress is kept in your iCloud account and comes back when you reinstall or sign in on a new iPhone or iPad.

How do I start again? Go to the Me tab and tap Reset all progress.

A word or tone looks wrong. Please tell us. Every phrase is checked, but corrections from Cantonese speakers are always welcome.
